How to play

Steer your snake to eat the glowing food and grow longer. Hitting a wall, any snake's body, or another snake head-on knocks you out, and your body turns into food. The last snake moving wins. If the last snakes crash together, the longest one wins.

Online: friends open this page, type a name and tap Join. The first person who joined runs the game and chooses the settings. Moves travel live, so a steady connection helps.
