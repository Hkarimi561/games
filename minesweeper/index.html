How to play

Every racer gets the exact same minefield. The start square (outlined) is opened for you when the countdown ends. Numbers show how many mines touch that square. Open every safe square to finish; the fastest time wins.

Switch between Dig and Flag, or long-press a square to flag it. Tap a number whose mines are all flagged to open the squares around it.

Online: friends open this page, type a name and join. Anyone who has joined can start the race. Each player's time is measured on their own device.
