Game Night

Five quick games for your phone. Play against the computer, or make a room and send the link to friends. No sign-up.

Playing with friends

1

Open a game and tap Online. Choose Create a room.

2

Tap Invite friends and send them the link, or read out the 5-letter room code.

3

Keep the game open while you play. If the person who made the room closes it, the room ends.

Online rooms connect your devices directly using PeerJS. Nothing is stored on a server, and no account is needed.
