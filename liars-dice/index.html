How to play

Everyone has five dice hidden under a cup. On your turn, bid on how many dice of one face are on the whole table, counting everyone's dice, for example "four 5s". Each bid must be higher: more dice, or the same number of a higher face.

If you think the last bid is too high, call Liar!. All dice are shown. If the bid was wrong, the bidder loses a die; if it was right, the caller loses a die. Lose all your dice and you're out. Last player with dice wins.

With ones wild, every 1 counts as whatever face was bid, and you can't bid on 1s.

Online: each player opens this page, takes a seat, and the dice are rolled privately on each player's own device. Keep the page open during the game so your dice can be shown when someone calls Liar. Computer players are run by the first person seated.
