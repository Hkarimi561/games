How to play

Players take turns drawing. The drawer picks one of three words and draws it; everyone else tries to guess it before time runs out. Letters of the word appear as hints while the clock runs. No letters or numbers in your drawing!

Scoring: a faster correct guess earns more points (up to 100), and the drawer earns points for every player who guesses. Most points after the last round wins.

One device: everyone looks at the same screen and shouts guesses. The drawer taps "Someone guessed it" and picks who.

Online: everyone opens this page and joins. The drawing appears live on every screen and guesses are typed in. A correct guess is never shown to the others.
