Three-Way Dots

Dots & Boxes for 2 to 6 players. Close a box to claim it and go again.

Players
3

Changing players or board size starts a new game.

How to play

Take turns drawing one line between two neighbouring dots. If your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours and you draw again. When every box is taken, the player with the most boxes wins.

AI players: set any seat to Easy, Medium or Hard. Easy plays loosely, Medium never hands you a box if it can avoid it, and Hard also gives away the smallest chain when it's forced to.

Online: each friend opens this page on their own device, types a name and picks a seat. Empty seats can be filled with AI. The game starts when every seat is filled. AI moves are made by the browsers of the seated players, so keep at least one of you on the page.
