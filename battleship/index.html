How to play

Place five ships: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2). Ships can't overlap. Then take turns firing at a square on the enemy chart: a dot is a miss, a red X is a hit. Sink every enemy ship to win.

Online: both players open this page, take a seat, place ships and tap Ready. Your ship positions are saved privately, so your opponent can't see them. Keep the page open during the game so your side can answer incoming shots.
